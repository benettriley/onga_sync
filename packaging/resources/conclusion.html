INSTALLED

Open (or restart) your DAW. Everything is listed under the maker ONGA.

  • Logic Pro: Audio FX → Audio Units → ONGA
  • Ableton Live: Plug-Ins → VST3 or Audio Units → ONGA
  • Reaper, Bitwig, Studio One, Cubase: VST3 → ONGA

If something doesn’t appear, use your DAW’s “rescan plug-ins” option once.

To remove the suite, run Uninstall ONGA Sync.pkg from the disk image. It moves everything to the Trash.
