ONGA SYNC
The ONGA library · @VERSION@

ONGA Sync keeps every ONGA plug-in on this Mac installed and up to date.

  • Install any plug-in in one click: Audio Unit and VST3, plus the standalone app if you want it.
  • Updates show up as they land. Update one, or all of them at once.
  • Uninstall cleanly: everything goes to the Trash, your presets stay.
  • Sign in once. Your licences live in your ONGA account.

This installs /Applications/ONGA Sync.app. Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer.
