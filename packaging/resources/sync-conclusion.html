READY

Open ONGA Sync from your Applications folder, sign in, and pick your plug-ins.

ONGA Sync asks for your Mac password when it installs or removes a plug-in, because plug-ins live in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins where every DAW and user can see them.

Plug-ins show up in your DAW under the maker ONGA. Rescan once if one is missing.
