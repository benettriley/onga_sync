ONGA SYNC  -  the ONGA plug-in suite  -  @VERSION@
==================================================

INSTALL
  1. Quit your DAW.
  2. Double-click "Install ONGA Sync.pkg".
  3. Tick the plug-ins you want (all are ticked by default), and the standalone
     apps if you want them. Enter your Mac password when asked.
  4. Open your DAW. Everything is listed under the maker "ONGA".

  If macOS says the installer "can't be opened because it is from an
  unidentified developer": right-click (or Control-click) the .pkg, choose
  Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

  Older copies under earlier names are moved to the Trash so each plug-in is
  listed once. Sessions saved with them still open.

WHAT'S INSIDE
@PLUGIN_LINES@

  Audio Unit  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  VST3        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Apps        /Applications (optional)

  Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer.

UNINSTALL
  Double-click "Uninstall ONGA Sync.pkg". Every ONGA plug-in and app is moved
  to your Trash; nothing else is touched.
