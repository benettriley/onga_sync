ONGA SYNC  -  the ONGA plug-in library  -  suite @VERSION@
==========================================================

INSTALL
  1. Double-click "Install ONGA Sync.pkg".
  2. Open ONGA Sync from Applications and sign in.
  3. Pick a plug-in and click INSTALL, or UPDATE ALL when updates land.
     Enter your Mac password when asked.
  4. Open your DAW. Everything is listed under the maker "ONGA".

  If macOS says the installer "can't be opened because it is from an
  unidentified developer": right-click (or Control-click) the .pkg, choose
  Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

  Older copies under earlier names are moved to the Trash so each plug-in is
  listed once. Sessions saved with them still open.

THE LIBRARY
@PLUGIN_LINES@

  Audio Unit  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  VST3        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Apps        /Applications (optional: tick STANDALONE APPS in ONGA Sync)

  Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer.

OFFLINE
  No internet on the studio Mac? "Install ONGA Suite.pkg" (on the release
  page) installs every plug-in in one go.

UNINSTALL
  One plug-in: select it in ONGA Sync and click UNINSTALL.
  Everything: double-click "Uninstall ONGA.pkg". Every ONGA plug-in, app and
  ONGA Sync itself go to your Trash; your presets stay.
